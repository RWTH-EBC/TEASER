# Detailed multi family house: defaults and assumptions

Notes on `DetailedMultiFamilyHouse` (`geometry_data="detailed_multi_family_house"`,
`construction_data="detailed_mfh_standard"`): what was taken from the
colleague's data, what was assumed, and what is still open. State of branch
`mfh-archetype` (TEASER b0dd16e1/de7fd78f, BESMod f9aff01e), October 2026.

Source data: `D:\03_TEASER_dev\MFH_Archetyp`
- `Definition_Archetyp_MFH_Bauteilaufbauten_lme_VariantenCWirk.xlsx`
  (IWU/Zensus statistics, Kühn/Nikolowski half plan, layers of 1965/1990/2025,
  U- and g-values)
- `Modell_1990.idf` (EnergyPlus 24.2, 67 zones, room-wise)
- `Modell_1990_SIMPLIFIED.idf` (17 zones, one per flat)
- `Test_Spawn_mmu.mo` (hand-wired BESMod Spawn model, 60 zones)

## Decisions taken with the user

- Floor plan of Kühn/Nikolowski (the one `Modell_1990.idf` uses), not the
  15 x 10 m sketch.
- Two flats per staircase and storey; more flats = more staircases
  (`number_of_staircases`) or more storeys (`number_of_floors`).
- `net_leased_area_per_flat` scales the flats; `net_leased_area` is accepted
  as alternative (divided by the number of flats).
- ROM zoning `"building"` or `"flat"`, or any function of a room's `ZoneInfo`
  (room for more options later, e.g. by orientation).
- Spawn/EnergyPlus room-wise, one zone per staircase over all storeys.

- Rooms are boxes of their inner dimensions; walls and slabs are gaps between
  them. Faces across a gap ≤ max(inner wall, slab) are paired; leftovers are
  envelope or adiabatic "gap" surfaces (where walls meet).
- Room names `F{storey}_A{flat}_{Type}`, storey 0 = ground floor, flats
  numbered west to east from 1. Other zones: `Staircase{k}`, `Basement`,
  `Attic`.
- Orientation: living room and bedroom face south, all other rooms and the
  staircase face north (`rotation = 0`). The colleague's IDF has north axis
  −90°, i.e. living rooms facing west. `rotate_building(90)` turns the
  building clockwise, so its living rooms face west; it works before and
  after `generate_archetype` and is written to the IDF as north axis.
- Windows per room (width x height, sill), from the plan:
  - Living: balcony door 1.125 x 2.125 (sill 0.01 m, because EnergyPlus
    warns about a window on the wall edge) and a window 1.125 x 1.25 (sill 0.875)
  - Bedroom 2.25 x 1.25, Children 1.25 x 1.25, Bath 0.875 x 1.25,
    Kitchen 1.125 x 1.25, Hallway none (sills 0.875 m)
  - Staircase: 1.875 x 1.25 per storey, north facade
- Windows are not scaled with the rooms (`net_leased_area_per_flat`).
- Gable walls: outside air by default; `neighbour_buildings` (0, 1 = west,
  2, or a tuple of "west"/"east") makes them adiabatic. In the ROM they then
  become an InnerWall with the outer wall's layers. The colleague's IDF has
  both gable walls adiabatic.
- Balconies, roof overhangs, shading and inner doors (120 in the colleague's
  IDF) are not modelled.

## Scaling (`scale_plan`)

- `scaling = "length"` (default): only the room widths along the building's
  length stretch, the depth stays (as the colleague's larger 2025 variant).
- `scaling = "proportional"`: widths and depths stretch by the same factor.
- Staircase and walls are never scaled. Found by bisection to the exact
  flat area.

## Constructions (`TypeElements_DetailedMFH.json`)

Generated by `D:\03_TEASER_dev\mfh\scripts\make_detailed_mfh_json.py` from the
Excel sheets Bauphysik and Bauteile. New materials `detailed_mfh_*` in
`MaterialTemplates.json`.

- Age groups: 1965 → [0, 1978], 1990 → [1979, 1994], 2025 → [2021, 2100].
  Years 1995–2020 have no data and raise an error. TABULA/IWU construction
  data is not supported for this archetype.
- Windows: U = 3.392 / 2.85 / 0.951 W/(m²K), g = 0.594 / 0.705 / 0.55,
  frame fraction 0.3 (assumption), no shading.
- Element types: OuterWall, OuterWallCellar (0.24 m concrete, uninsulated),
  Rooftop (roof tiles only, the attic is unheated), GroundFloor (0.24 m
  concrete), InnerWall (partition), InnerWallApartment (between flats and to
  the staircase), Ceiling/Floor (between storeys), CeilingAttic (top slab),
  FloorCellar (basement slab).
- Insulation thicknesses (rock wool): outer wall 0 / 0.015 / 0.145 m,
  top slab 0.025 / 0.06 / 0.19 m, basement slab 0.02 / 0.04 / 0.165 m.
  The IDF material "Steinwolle 165mm" has 0.19 m; 0.165 m was used.
- Surface coefficients as in TABULA: inner convection 2.7 (walls) /
  1.7 W/(m²K) (horizontal), inner and outer radiation 5, outer convection 20.
- Plain element types are listed before FloorCellar, CeilingAttic,
  OuterWallCellar and InnerWallApartment in the JSON, as TEASER matches keys
  by prefix; `_load` checks that the exact type was found.

## Unheated spaces in the ROM

- Basement, staircases and attic are unheated and not TEASER thermal zones.
- Each heated room's element towards them becomes a horizontal Rooftop
  stand-in to the outside air with U' = b · U (extra resistance layer),
  outer convection 20, outer radiation ≈ 0, no solar absorption.
- b = (t_room − t_u) / (t_room − t_outside) from a steady-state heat balance
  of all unheated spaces together at the design conditions
  (t_outside 261.15 K, TEASER's t_ground), coupled to the heated rooms, the
  outside air, the ground and each other.
- Air change of the unheated spaces (`unheated_air_change_rates`): staircase
  0.5, basement 0.5, attic 2.5 1/h (DIN EN 12831-1 Table 5, "undicht").
- Results 1990, 5 storeys: staircase 13.7 °C, basement 12.3 °C,
  attic −9.9 °C at design conditions.
- Walls and slabs between heated rooms are halved (each side gets its half
  of the layers).

## Use conditions and set temperatures

- All zones use TEASER's "Living" use conditions, without AHU.
- `room_t_set_nominal`: 20 °C for all rooms, 24 °C for the bath.
- Zone set temperature (ROM) = volume-weighted mean of its rooms.

## Heat load

- Room-wise (`calc_room_heat_loads`): UA to outside incl. stand-ins times
  (t_room − t_outside), plus normative infiltration of the room's volume,
  plus the exchange with heated neighbours at a lower design temperature.
  Zone heat load = sum of its rooms.
- `area_gf` = footprint, `area_rt` = attic roof area (e.g. for PV in BESMod).
- 1990, 5 storeys, plan as drawn: 649 m², 35.9 kW. 1965: 72.8 W/m².
  2025 at 77 m²/flat: 27.4 W/m², length 19.67 m.

## EnergyPlus export (`export_idf`)

- Every room is a zone, each staircase one zone over all storeys, basement
  and attic one zone each. Unheated zones are unconditioned and get
  ZoneInfiltration with `unheated_air_change_rates`.
- Heated rooms get no infiltration in the IDF (Spawn sets it from Modelica).
- Inside convection TARP, outside DOE-2, 12 time steps per hour,
  ground temperature = TEASER's t_ground for all months.
- Building north axis = `rotation`.
- Verified with EnergyPlus 26.1 (after transition from 24.2): 0 severe
  errors; areas and volumes match TEASER.

## BESMod export with Spawn (`export_besmod(export_with_spawn=True)`)

- `SpawnMultiZone` (BESMod): one Spawn ThermalZone per heated room; the
  unheated zones are simulated by EnergyPlus as free floating.
- `RoomWiseProfiles`: internal gains per room = the zone's gains per m²
  times the room's floor area; set temperature = `room_t_set_nominal`, set
  back like the zone's heating profile; natural ventilation = the zone's
  normative infiltration (0.5 1/h), constant.
- Radiative share of the internal gains = 1 − the yearly convective share.
- Only TEASERHeatLoadCalculation, HeatPumpSimple and GasBoilerBuildingOnly
  get a Spawn variant (no mechanical ventilation in BESMod's Spawn buildings).
- Spawn needs a short working path (Windows MAX_PATH): building the FMU
  fails under long temp paths. Run it in e.g. `D:\03_TEASER_dev\mfh`.

## Inconsistencies in the colleague's data

- Two different floor plans (15 x 10 m sketch vs. Kühn/Nikolowski 16.4 m).
- Storey height 2.7 m (detailed IDF) vs. 2.44 m (simplified IDF) vs. zone
  height 2.5 m.
- "Steinwolle 165mm" has a thickness of 0.19 m in the IDF.
- Flat area 69.7 m² (centreline) vs. 65.5 m² (net).
- Simplified IDF: zone names of the staircase (TRH) and a flat (W2) are
  swapped.

## Open points

- Confirm with the colleague: roof tilt 30° vs. ~23°, orientation (living
  rooms south vs. west), frame fraction 0.3, slab 0.21 m, inner doors.
- Spawn needs 13.6 % less heat than the ROM (14 days January, 1990,
  5 storeys, zoning "flat", days 5–14: ROM 4229 kWh, Spawn 3655 kWh; the
  single family house differs by about 4 %). Not investigated yet.
  Suspects: ROM's dynamic natural ventilation vs. constant 0.5 1/h in Spawn,
  temperature-factor stand-ins vs. free-floating unheated zones in
  EnergyPlus, solar distribution and window films.
- Run time 14 days: ROM 33 s, Spawn (60 zones) 159 s.
- More zoning options (e.g. by orientation) and a construction data set for
  1995–2020.
